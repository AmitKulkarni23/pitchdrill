# Design System: PitchDrill

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Shopping Channel Where Anyone Presents"**

PitchDrill looks like live broadcast graphics: the channel bug, lower-third name straps, price bursts, an on-air countdown clock and a TV-listings program guide. Every surface reads as a broadcast in progress, with the selling and the clock always in frame.

Large saturated studio fields own whole regions; type is set in condensed heavy capitals for straps and clocks and in a plain workhorse sans for reading. Motion is broadcast motion: straps wipe in from their colored lead edge, clocks tick, tallies fill.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Saturated studio color owns whole regions; no washed-out neutrals.
- Broadcast graphics are the component language (bug, straps, bursts, clock, guide).
- Time is always visible and monumental.
- One decision per step.

## Colors

[to be resolved during implementation] Strategy: committed studio color field plus a full broadcast palette (studio ground, white type, price-burst yellow for the primary action and prices, on-air red reserved for LIVE and the last ten seconds).

### Named Rules
**The On-Air Rule.** Red means live or about to end. It is never decoration.

## Typography

[to be resolved during implementation] A condensed heavy grotesk for straps, clocks and headlines (tabular figures for every clock), a workhorse sans for body text.

## Layout

The page is a broadcast frame: a top bar with the channel bug and the next-round clock, a main "on air" area, and lower-third bands. The program guide is a time-by-day grid on desktop and a single-day channel strip on phones.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat broadcast layers: depth comes from stacked color plates (a strap over a field), not soft shadows.

## Shapes

Straight-edged plates with a slanted lead edge on straps and role tabs; no rounded cards.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep a clock or time on every surface where a round is involved.
- **Do** label illustrated rounds as examples.

### Don't:
- **Don't** invent audience numbers, ratings, testimonials or "rounds played" counts.
- **Don't** show the buyer persona anywhere a seller or spectator can see it.
